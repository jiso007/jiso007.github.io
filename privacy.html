Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains what data JISO handles when it manages Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising for its clients, and how that data is used and protected.

Who this covers

JISO provides advertising management to businesses ("clients"). It covers data from clients' Meta ad accounts that clients have shared with JISO, and contact details of the people at those clients we work with.

What we collect

  • Ad account data through Meta's Marketing API: campaign, ad set and ad settings, budgets, targeting settings, creative assets and performance results such as spend, impressions, clicks and conversions.
  • Pixel and dataset statistics needed to judge tracking health, such as event counts and match quality. We do not receive the underlying browsing data of individual visitors.
  • Customer lists, only when a client chooses to provide one for a custom audience. These are hashed before upload to Meta and used for that client's ads only.
  • Business contact details of client staff, such as name and email, to send reports and approval requests.

How we use it

Only to manage, report on and improve the client's own advertising. We do not sell data, share it with other clients, or use one client's data to target another client's audiences.

Who we share it with

  • Meta, as required to run the client's ads.
  • Service providers that host our software or process data on our behalf, including AI model providers used to analyse performance and draft recommendations. They may use the data only to provide that service.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Security and retention

Access tokens are encrypted at rest and never written to logs. Data is kept while a client works with us and deleted within 30 days after access ends, except the change log, which we keep for the period agreed with the client so that every change remains accountable.

Your choices and data deletion

A client can remove JISO's access at any time in Meta Business Settings, under Partners. To have data deleted, contact us at the address below; we confirm deletion within 30 days. People who are not clients can ask what data, if any, we hold about them in the same way.
